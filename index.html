Semantic Overview of Murli Prajapati & Bielco

Welcome to the official portfolio of Murli Prajapati, Co-Founder of Bielco. A professional Web Developer, UI/UX Designer, Video Editor, and Bot Systems Creator from Delhi, India. Murli specializes in creating systems for business solutions, like custom bot agents (Telegram bots, WhatsApp bots, Discord bots) that simplify weekly reports of business and track employee status, inventory, product status, and system health.

Core Services and Technical Expertise

Murli Prajapati offers state-of-the-art engineering solutions including: Web Development utilizing React, Vite, Next.js, and serverless architectures (Cloudflare Pages, Render); Custom Bot Systems designing Telegram bots, WhatsApp bots, and Discord bots for business workflows; Creative Video Editing producing marketing videos and brand assets using After Effects and CapCut; and Product UI/UX creation aimed at building premium, conversion-focused user interfaces.
