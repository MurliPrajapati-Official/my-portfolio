About Murli Prajapati

Murli Prajapati is a web developer, UI/UX designer, video editor and bot systems creator from Delhi, India, and co-founder of Bielco (bielco.in). He builds full websites in React, Next.js and TypeScript, designs conversion-focused interfaces in Figma, and creates Telegram, WhatsApp and Discord bots that summarise weekly business reports, employee status, inventory, products and system health.
